Decorative ornament for a text box in a children's picture book. Paint only the decoration (a frame, border, corner flourishes, banner, scroll or background shapes); the book's real text is printed on top of it later.

Style: Soft watercolor, warm light.

Avoid: no text, letters or words in the image

An ornate gold storybook frame with curling green vines at the corners.

Composition: landscape, about 2:1, filling the whole image. The centre must stay completely plain and empty: the book's text is printed there later. Keep the ornament to the edges and corners, or soft and low-contrast behind the text area. No letters, words, numbers or text of any kind.

Background: plain pure white (#FFFFFF) with nothing else: no paper texture, shadow, vignette or tint. The empty centre is the same pure white.
